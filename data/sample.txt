F-1 students may work on-campus up to 20 hours per week while school is in
session and full-time during official breaks. Off-campus employment generally
requires prior authorization. Curricular Practical Training (CPT) allows work
that is an integral part of an established curriculum. Optional Practical
Training (OPT) allows up to 12 months of work authorization related to the
student's field of study, with a possible 24-month extension for qualifying
STEM degrees.
