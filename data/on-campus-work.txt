On-campus employment is work that F-1 students whose status is Active in SEVIS may apply for. On-campus employment is specific to work that takes place on campus or at an off-campus location that is [educationally affiliated](https://www.ice.gov/sevis/employment#_1_7) with the school. Examples of on-campus employment include working at a university bookstore or cafeteria.
Active F-1 students may apply for on-campus employment up to 30 days before the start of classes. In order to apply, talk to your DSO. If approved, your DSO will provide you with a letter of approval. Take this letter from your DSO and a letter of approval from your employer to [apply for a Social Security Number](http://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/steps-for-obtaining-a-social-security-number) (SSN). All students who wish to work must apply for a Social Security Number.  
If you participate in on-campus employment, you may not work more than 20 hours per week when school is in session. If you have additional questions, please visit the [F-1 Student On-Campus page on ICE.gov](http://www.ice.gov/sevis/employment#tab0).
